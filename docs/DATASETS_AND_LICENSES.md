# Dataset acquisition and terms

No dataset archive is included in this repository. `code/datasets_r1.py`
downloads each one from its upstream source into `data_cache/`, parses it with
torch and numpy only, and records the SHA-256 of every archive it fetched, so
an independent run can confirm it obtained the same bytes.

## MNIST

- Source: <http://yann.lecun.com/exdb/mnist/>, mirrored at
  <https://ossci-datasets.s3.amazonaws.com/mnist/>
- Reference: LeCun, Bottou, Bengio, and Haffner (1998), *Gradient-based
  learning applied to document recognition*.
- Redistribution: no archive is redistributed here; consult the official source
  for applicable terms.

- Source: <https://github.com/zalandoresearch/fashion-mnist>

## 20 Newsgroups

- Source: <https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/113/twenty+newsgroups>
- Archive: `20news-bydate.tar.gz`
- Dataset DOI: <https://doi.org/10.24432/C5C323>
- UCI license: CC BY 4.0.
- Preparation: the standard train and test subsets are used. Documents are
  hashed into a 20,000-item vocabulary and truncated or padded to 96 content
  slots, and a prepended class token brings the attention sequence to 97.
- Masking: padded positions are masked **before** the normalizer rather than
  after it, so they receive no attention mass under any of the seven
  normalizers, and attention density is computed over valid keys only. An
  earlier round of this benchmark left those slots unmasked and computed
  density over all slots; correcting that is one of the reasons this round
  exists.
